## Design Requirements

- A stable, level base that holds the PVC tower and minimizes vibration
- Parts that can be laser cut from sheet wood and assembled without glue where possible

## Laser-Cut Base (AutoCAD)

Interlocking rings with a Ø1.90 in bore grip the PVC tower, and tabbed ribs slot into them
to make a rigid base (R1.5 in rings, 5.0 in overall span, 4.0 in ribs).
