## Challenge

Vintage steel frames weren't designed for modern crate engines. The mounting points, tank
clearance and drivetrain alignment all had to be worked out from scratch, with the build
designed around parts that were never made to fit together.

## What I built

- **Engine mounts:** fabricated to locate the crate engine in the frame and line up the drivetrain.
- **Gas tank mounts:** made so the tank clears the new engine.
- **Gear / drivetrain mounts:** fabricated to fit the gear and drive components to the frame.
